# Hi, I'm Yangda.

I’m currently a Research Fellow at [Iliad](https://www.iliad.ac/). I study deep learning theory and safety, with a particular interest in how
the geometry and dynamics of training determine what neural networks learn. My
current work uses tensor networks as mathematically tractable models of deep
learning.

Before transitioning to safety research, I studied pure mathematics and computer
science at the Australian National University, where I wrote my
[honours thesis](/assets/pdf/Thesis_YB.pdf) on categorical braid group actions.
I am also interested in AI for mathematics and Lean, as well as technical AI governance.

Outside of research, I enjoy reading and the outdoors! I am currently training for a marathon and gotten back into tennis again. Growing up, I played the piano, [euphonium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euphonium), and trombone.
