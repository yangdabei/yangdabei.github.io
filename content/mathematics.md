## Expository writing

### Honours thesis : [_A Braid Group Action on Category $\mathcal{O}$_](/assets/pdf/Thesis_YB.pdf)

In this thesis, I give a self-contained exposition of a braid group action on
the bounded derived category of category $\mathcal{O}$ due to Rouquier. This structure plays an important role in the representation theory of Lie algebras and has connections to
modern geometry. I discuss Soergel's original work on category O,
Stroppel-–Mazorchuk shuffling functors, and the Elias-–Williamson diagrammatic
category of Soergel bimodules.
