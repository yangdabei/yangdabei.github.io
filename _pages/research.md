### honours thesis
##### [___A Braid Group Action on Category $\mathcal{O}$___](https://yangdabei.github.io/assets/pdf/Thesis_YB.pdf) 
<!-- <div class="caption">
    2025
</div> -->



In this thesis, I provide a self-contained exposition of a braid group action on the bounded derived category of category $\mathcal{O}$ due to Rouquier. This structure is of great importance in the representation theory of Lie algebras and has connections to modern geometry. I touch upon Soergel's original work on category $\mathcal{O}$, Stroppel--Mazorchuk's shuffling functors, and the Elias--Williamson diagrammatic category of Soergel bimodules.
