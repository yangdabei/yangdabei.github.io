Hi, I'm Yangda!

I am currently a research assistant at UNSW investigating the interpretability of equation discovery machine learning algorithms with applications to climate change under [Dr. Sanaa Hobeichi](https://www.unsw.edu.au/staff/sanaa-hobeichi).

My broad interest is getting technology to do exactly what we want it to do. I'm inspired by the neuroscience of learning and how it can inform the development of artificial intelligence. Attending the [Human-Aligned AI Summer School](https://humanaligned.ai/2024/) in July 2024 first got me thinking about interpretable machine learning and AI alignment, which I'm now transitioning into through the [TARA program](https://www.taraprogram.org/) and the [Iliad intensive](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/b9bhm2iypgkCNppv4/announcement-iliad-intensive-iliad-fellowship) (beginning June). I'm also interested in applications of machine learning to mathematical research. Previously, I was contracted by the Australian Signals Directorate to red-team the [Swiss Post e-voting system](https://www.post.ch/en/about-us/profile/swiss-post-and-politics/swiss-post-in-the-digital-world/e-voting-electronic-vote-casting-for-switzerland) for cryptographic vulnerabilities.

I recently graduated from the Australian National University with First Class Honours, where I majored in pure mathematics and computer science, with a minor in physics. I wrote my [honours thesis](https://yangdabei.github.io/assets/pdf/Thesis_YB.pdf) on a braid group action on category $$\mathcal{O}$$, co-supervised by [Dr. Asilata Bapat](https://asilatabapat.org/) and [Dr. Leonardo Maltoni](https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~leonardo.maltoni/).
