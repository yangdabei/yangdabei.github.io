Hi, I'm Yangda!

I am an incoming Research Fellow at [PIBBSS x Iliad](https://www.iliad.ac/). Before transitioning to AI safety research, I did some climate research on equation discovery and added value. I studied pure mathematics and computer science at the Australian National University where I wrote my [honours thesis](https://yangdabei.github.io/assets/pdf/Thesis_YB.pdf) on categorical braid group actions. Other areas I'm interested in includes: AI for mathematics, learning theory and training dynamics, and neuroscience.

Recently, I started marathon training as well as getting back into playing tennis and volleyball. Music has also been a big part of my life, studying piano, euphonium, and trombone growing up.
