Mathematics

My mathematical interests span representation theory, algebraic geometry, topology, and category theory, shaped by the excellent lecturers with whom I took graduate-level courses during the final two years of my undergraduate degree.

Expository writing

Honours thesis : A Braid Group Action on Category O\mathcal{O}O

In this thesis, I give a self-contained exposition of a braid group action on the bounded derived category of category O\mathcal{O}O due to Rouquier. This structure plays an important role in the representation theory of Lie algebras and has connections to modern geometry. I discuss Soergel’s original work on category O, Stroppel-–Mazorchuk shuffling functors, and the Elias-–Williamson diagrammatic category of Soergel bimodules.
